How it works

  1. 1

    Pick a partner

    Choose a scenario, like a coffee date or asking for a second date, and a practice partner. Every partner comes in a woman and a man version.

  2. 2

    5-minute date

    Chat with an AI partner who acts like a real person: warm, shy, bored or skeptical. Text first; voice is planned.

  3. 3

    Scorecard

    See your talk share, six skill scores, 3 wins, 3 fixes and one replay moment. Honest and kind, always with one thing to try next.

  4. 4

    Level up

    Average 3.5 or better to unlock the next partner. Each level is harder and trains a different skill.

FAQ

Are the practice partners real people?

No. Every practice partner is AI. They're written to act like real people, warm, shy, bored or skeptical, so the practice feels real, but you're never talking to a human. If you sincerely ask a partner whether it's AI, it will tell you.

Who is Warmup for?

Adults 18 and over who feel awkward on dates or in conversation and want low-stakes reps. Warmup is 18+ only and never for minors. Everything stays PG-13, with no sexual content.

Will this get me more dates?

We can't promise that, and we won't. Warmup is practice: it helps you rehearse conversation skills and get honest feedback. What happens on real dates is up to you and the other person.

Is this pickup lines or “rizz” scripts?

No. Warmup teaches real skills: curiosity, listening, humor, storytelling, confidence and respect. It never teaches manipulation, pressure or lying about yourself. The hardest level is about respecting a soft no.
